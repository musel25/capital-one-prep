# Banco de preguntas y repreguntas

Preguntas originales de práctica en inglés; los temas se inspiran en testimonios públicos, no son preguntas garantizadas. Usa hechos de tus experiencias. En mocks: elige la historia después de escuchar la pregunta y no leas un texto.

## S1 — Trabajo en equipo

- Tell me about a time you helped a team deliver a difficult project.
- What did you own personally?
- Which dependency put delivery at risk?
- What did another teammate contribute?
- How did you know the team had succeeded?

## S2 — Desacuerdo

- Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate and how you handled it.
- Why was the other person’s position reasonable?
- What did you say in the first conversation?
- Did you change your mind about anything?
- What happened to the working relationship afterward?

## S3 — Pedir ayuda a otro equipo

- Tell me about a time you asked another team for expertise your team lacked.
- How did you recognize you needed help?
- What had you tried before asking?
- How did you make the request easy to act on?
- How did you share the knowledge afterward?

## S4 — Cuestionar una práctica

- Tell me about a time you challenged an existing way of working.
- Why had the old process existed?
- What risk did your proposal introduce?
- How did you persuade someone who disagreed?
- What would you keep from the original approach?

## S5 — Aprender con poco contexto

- Tell me about a time you had to learn something unfamiliar quickly.
- How did you decide what not to learn?
- How did you test your understanding?
- Who or what corrected a misunderstanding?
- What could you do independently afterward?

## S6 — Error y recuperación

- Tell me about a mistake you made and how you responded.
- When did you first know something was wrong?
- Who did you tell, and when?
- What was the actual impact?
- What evidence shows the prevention step helped?

## S7 — Uso responsable de IA

- Tell me about a time you used AI to help with a technical task and how you verified the result.
- What did the tool get wrong or leave uncertain?
- What did you verify without trusting the generated explanation?
- What information did you avoid sharing?
- When would you decide not to use AI?

## S8 — Ambigüedad y prioridades

- Tell me about a time you had to make progress with unclear requirements or competing priorities.
- Which assumption was riskiest?
- Who had authority to choose the priority?
- What did you defer, and why?
- How did you communicate what would not be delivered?

## Repreguntas transversales

## Program motivation and project depth

- Introduce yourself and explain what led you toward software engineering.
- Why Capital One and why the Technology Development Program? Connect a verified aspect of the program with your actual learning goals.
- Walk through one real project and trace a request through its components.
- What did you implement personally, what alternative did you consider, and what would you revisit?

From day 3, the website draws questions before you choose a story. Give a 90–120-second initial answer, then reveal follow-ups. The draw order is session-only; record the question and story in private notes. Practice two or three questions inside the 30-minute block. Do not force a memorized story onto an unrelated question.

## Cross-topic follow-ups

- What exactly did you do, rather than the team as a whole?
- What alternative did you seriously consider?
- What evidence supported your choice at the time?
- What was the constraint you could not change?
- What did you tell the stakeholder when you were uncertain?
- Which result did you observe directly?
- What remains unknown about the outcome?
- What would you do differently in the same situation now?

## Preguntas que puedes adaptar para el entrevistador

- How do engineers on this team review and validate changes made with AI tools?
- What does a useful first contribution from a new engineer usually look like?
- How does the team clarify ambiguous product requirements?
- How do new engineers get feedback on technical decisions?
