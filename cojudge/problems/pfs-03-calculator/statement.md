## Learn

Numbers do not need quotes. Python can do math:

| Sign | Meaning | Example | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `+` | plus | `print(2 + 3)` | `5` |
| `-` | minus | `print(9 - 4)` | `5` |
| `*` | times | `print(6 * 7)` | `42` |

Be careful: `print("2 + 3")` has quotes, so it shows the text `2 + 3`, not `5`.
