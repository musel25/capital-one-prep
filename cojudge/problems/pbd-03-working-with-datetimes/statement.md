# 03. Working with datetimes

[Course list](/?course=pybites-datetimes-timezones) · [Original bite](https://pybitesplatform.com/bites/working-with-datetimes/)

Return datetimes from get_hundred_days_end(start=datetime(2017, 3, 30)): start + 100 days. Return whole elapsed days from get_days_between(start=datetime(2016, 12, 19), end=datetime(2018, 5, 8)). Inputs are naive datetimes at midnight. Negative intervals are allowed.

1. We kicked off our 100 Days of Code project on March 30th, 2017. Calculate what date we finished the full 100 days!
1. *PyBites* was founded on the 19th of December 2016. We're attending our first PyCon together on May 8th, 2018. Can you calculate how many days from PyBites' inception to our first PyCon meet up?
